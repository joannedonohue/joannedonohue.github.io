SECTION4 / PRODUCT STRATEGY SPRINT

Armoire.
Closet of the future.

Armoire is a women’s clothing rental subscription service. This product strategy case study examined how it could stand out in a crowded market through a clearer customer focus, more visible personalization, and stronger customer engagement. I analyzed its competitive position and developed recommendations for the Section4 Product Strategy Sprint.

1 of 4

Selected as one of four standout projects to be presented from a cohort of roughly 500 participants.

I completed the Product Strategy Sprint at Section, then known as Section4, the business education platform founded by Scott Galloway. The course was taught by Professor Adam Alter of NYU Stern, whose work connects marketing and psychology.

I applied the course frameworks to competition beyond direct substitutes: customer confidence, time, attention, and data. My recommendations focused on making Armoire’s personalization more visible, reducing friction in customer feedback, and building local community.
