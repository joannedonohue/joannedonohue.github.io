My role in the broader measurement program.

As a Senior Brand Analyst, I worked across TV and digital media investment, using geo-based incrementality tests, ghost ads, and causal impact models. I partnered with measurement vendors and Expedia’s Data Science team to connect evidence to media decisions.

The broader program included TV attribution and neuroanalytics inputs for the in-house programmatic buying platform. Reporting moved from monthly to weekly, and the work saved approximately 15% of non-working media capital. These are program-level outcomes, not claimed results of either individual case study.

What this brings to AI product work.

A tool can be used frequently and still fail to improve a decision. My measurement background pushes me to define the baseline, identify the behavior we want to change, and decide what evidence would justify continued investment.
