EXPEDIA / BRAND ANALYTICS

Beyond attribution.
Into causation.

The scope.

As a Senior Brand Analyst at Expedia, I worked on marketing effectiveness across TV and digital, including A/B testing, causal inference, and incrementality.

The question.

A conversion after an ad is not proof that the ad caused it. The product and business question is what changed because of the intervention, compared with what would have happened anyway.

My contribution.

My work included experimentation, ghost-ad approaches, and creative optimization with a neuroanalytics partner. I connected analytical findings to decisions about marketing investment and creative.

What I bring forward.

For an AI commerce product, adoption alone is not the finish line. I want an evaluation plan that separates a compelling demo from a tool that improves customer decisions and business outcomes.
