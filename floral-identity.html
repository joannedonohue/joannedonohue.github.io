ART & DESIGN / A PERSONAL IDENTITY

Serious work.
Unexpected expression.

The starting point.

A red floral dress, a pink bow, and personal photographs. I wanted a professional portfolio that felt like me: analytical, creative, and comfortable being a little unexpected.

The translation.

The dress’s florals became oversized blooms. Pink, orange, and deep green became the palette. An AI-assisted editorial portrait anchors the page, with glass surfaces and animated butterflies giving it a sense of life.

The working experience.

Big typography creates a clear entry point. A horizontal showcase opens into product, art, and community. Motion can be paused, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
