THE HUMAN BEHIND THE ROADMAP

Hi, I’m Joanne.
Analytical mind. Creative instinct.

I’m a product manager with a background in ecommerce, analytics, and experimentation. My work has taken me through Mars, Expedia, and investing at Sonoran Ridge Capital.

I like connecting dots across disciplines: customer needs, commercial outcomes, technical possibilities, and thoughtful design. I can talk strategy, dig into the data, and build to learn.

Away from work, you’ll find me making art, exploring desert trails, or following my curiosity somewhere new.

I’m originally from Bulgaria and grew up in the Toronto area and San Diego. I love exploring the world and different cultures with my husband, Nathan, and our terrier, Winston.
