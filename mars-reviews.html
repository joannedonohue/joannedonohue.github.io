The business question.

A star rating tells us how a product was rated. Review text helps explain why. Looking across the category lets a team investigate whether a complaint is specific to one product, common to a format, or part of a broader customer need.

My role and the product judgment.

Build a useful insight tool.

I built the tool to reduce the time needed to turn review text into usable customer insight. The resulting analysis helped inform a $5M packaging decision.

Keep the evidence visible.

For this portfolio demo, I made it possible to filter the category, compare brands, and inspect sample comments. Those interface choices illustrate how I would make the signals understandable and actionable today.

How I would evaluate this product today.

  • Label quality: compare sentiment and themes with a human-labeled sample, including mixed sentiment and ambiguous language.
  • Coverage: inspect review volume, duplicate text, brand mix, product format, and changes over time.
  • Decision quality: confirm that a recurring signal survives closer review before treating it as a product problem.
  • Business usefulness: measure time to a usable insight and whether the analysis improves the team’s decision.

These are proposed evaluation practices for a modern version. The historical model, training process, accuracy, and exact original dashboard design are not specified here.
