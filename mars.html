MARS / PRODUCT OWNERSHIP

Connecting the
digital shelf.

The scope.

My product work at Mars focused on ecommerce analytics for Amazon, connecting sales, marketing, and inventory information so teams could make better commercial decisions.

My contribution.

I owned product priorities and worked with a growing BI team on delivery. The work included Power BI dashboards, shared data models, and collaboration across business and technical stakeholders.

The product problem.

Useful analytics require more than a dashboard. Teams need consistent definitions, trusted inputs, and a clear connection between the information shown and the decision they need to make.

What I bring forward.

This experience shapes how I approach AI products: start with the decision, understand the data underneath it, and make the output useful in the customer’s workflow.
