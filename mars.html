Metric definitions & demo assumptions

Sales and units represent synthetic retailer sell-through, not Mars revenue. Year-over-year compares the selected weeks with equivalent prior-year weeks. Attributed media sales are a subset of total sales in this fixture. ROAS uses attributed sales; it is not a causal return estimate. Click-through rate = clicks ÷ impressions. Conversion rate = attributed orders ÷ clicks. In-stock, content compliance, and Buy Box rates use eligible SKU-week observations. Buy Box applies only to Amazon in this demo.

All values are fabricated. Retailer names indicate the historical deployment scope; these numbers do not describe their actual performance. Weeks end June 29–September 14, 2026. No live data connections or forecasting models are running.

The product problem.

Account teams needed to move from spreadsheet reporting to governed self-serve analytics. The challenge was both technical and human: unify data and KPI definitions, then make the platform useful enough to become part of the way teams worked.

What I owned.

The engagement and operating model.

I owned the deployment roadmap, governance, and operating model from needs assessment through post-launch support. I directed delivery across business stakeholders, technical teams, and vendor partners in two-week Agile sprints.

The adoption work.

I used stakeholder interviews, design sprints, persona mapping, and OKRs to shape the product. A training library, office hours, and release notes helped account teams make the shift to self-serve analytics.

I led the re-architecture from legacy on-premises systems to Azure Databricks and Unity Catalog with IT and data architecture partners. The platform had a roadmap toward natural-language AI querying. That was a future direction, not a claim that conversational AI was already deployed.

Category context, not just our own sales.

Earlier in my Mars role, I led vendor evaluation and enterprise rollout of Stackline, including a five-vendor selection process and integration work with Stackline engineers. I also built a Category Dashboard using Stackline and Nielsen data to support product innovation business cases. These experiences shaped how I connected retailer workflows with the data needed to answer them.

The outcome.

RBS expanded beyond the Amazon account team to four additional major retailers, moved account teams toward governed analytics, and earned the Mars Five Principles Award. It was central to Mars’ broader $1.2B digital transformation. That figure describes the transformation’s scale, not my project budget or attributed revenue.
