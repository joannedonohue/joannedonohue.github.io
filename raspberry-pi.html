01 / THE IDEA

From the screen
to my desk.

I wanted to build something I could hold, assemble, and make my own. This Raspberry Pi project brings together my interest in AI, playful interfaces, and the animals I care for at the wildlife rescue.

The hardware is assembled. Next, I want to turn it into three small experiences: a Pomodoro timer, a trivia game, and an AI companion that saves useful output to my Obsidian knowledge base.

This is an ongoing build. The app screens and workflows below describe the intended experience; the apps are not built yet.
