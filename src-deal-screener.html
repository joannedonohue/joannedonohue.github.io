03 / THE PRODUCT DECISIONS

A recommendation with receipts.

Separate the mandate from the model.

My original Python repository loads a Pitchbook-style CSV, applies five weighted startup criteria, and generates a deal brief. It is an anonymized sample. This page adapts the workflow to established service-business acquisitions, using SRC’s public thesis.

Show uncertainty before a score.

A missing net-income figure cannot establish net margin. I made missing fields visible and separated financial fit from customer risk, team quality, and transition readiness. Passing the first screen earns a conversation, not an investment approval.

Make the output useful.

The next step is a focused document request and owner conversation. Valuation, debt capacity, working capital, and quality of earnings remain outside this first screen. A production version would add source citations, access controls, and analyst review.

Inspect the demo rules

Revenue and EBITDA must fall inside the inclusive published ranges. Net income divided by revenue must exceed 15%. Location must be in the Southwest. Any known miss gives “Pass”; otherwise missing required data gives “Request data”; otherwise the result is “Advance to diligence.”

Recurring revenue below 50%, a customer above 25% of revenue, and a transition outside 3–12 months trigger separate flags. The 50% and 25% cutoffs are demo assumptions, not published SRC criteria. Qualitative claims always require verification.
