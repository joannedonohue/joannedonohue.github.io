Less busywork.
More useful answers.

I help run Sonoran Ridge Capital, a private investment firm focused on investing in and acquiring businesses. I lead automation and market research to understand how each business makes money, who its customers are, and where technology can create a competitive advantage. Using Python, Google Colab, and Attio, I turn that research into repeatable sourcing, outreach, and evaluation workflows.
