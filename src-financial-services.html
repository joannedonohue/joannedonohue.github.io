SONORAN RIDGE CAPITAL / FINANCIAL SERVICES

From a tax question
to an advisor conversation.

I built an intent-based marketing tool for a boutique financial services firm in our portfolio at Sonoran Ridge Capital. We serve high-net-worth business owners with specialized tax-planning needs. I designed the tool around two decisions. The owner decides whether our expertise fits their situation. The wealth advisor decides which content the team can publish.

Follow the intent signals ↓See the approval process

Audience intent · Content strategy · Advisor approval

01 / INTENT-BASED MARKETING

Help the right business owners find us on their own.

An owner considering a business sale might start with “When should I bring a wealth advisor into the conversation?” That question guides both the topic we cover and where we show up. An owner can find a useful answer in search, watch an advisor explain it on YouTube, and decide whether to reach out.

When the owner asks to meet

The consultation form passes the selected service and available referral source to Slant CRM. The meeting brief gives the advisor a starting question: “What prompted you to explore tax planning for your business now?” The owner gets to explain what matters to them. Their prospect record stays outside the marketing agents’ workspace.

02 / COMPLIANCE & PUBLICATION

Three agents review the work. The advisor decides what goes public.

I set up the agents in a SharePoint folder firewalled from client information. They work with approved source material and aggregate marketing signals. Their outputs are private ideas and drafts for the team, including blog posts, YouTube video outlines, and LinkedIn posts.
