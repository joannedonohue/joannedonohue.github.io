SONORAN RIDGE CAPITAL / FINANCIAL SERVICES

From search signal
to advisor review.

I designed a research workflow for a financial services firm to understand what prospective clients wanted to know. A marketing agent uses that evidence to propose content. A wealth advisor decides whether the final wording can go public.

Product design · Answer engine optimization · Human review

RESEARCH & RECOMMENDATIONS

Which question deserves a better answer?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) starts with a question a prospective client might ask. I designed the CMO agent to trace that question through the firm’s YouTube videos, blog posts, and other publications. It reads the video transcripts to find the advisor’s original wording, then compares it with the written coverage. When traffic points to growing interest in a topic, the agent flags a specific edit for the advisor to review.

Example · A video answers a question the blog leaves open

Suppose Google Analytics shows more AI referrals to a blog post about preparing for a first advisor meeting. Clarity shows readers repeatedly clicking the document list. In a YouTube transcript, the agent finds the advisor walking through which documents to bring and why. It flags that passage and proposes adding a short checklist to the post. The advisor checks whether the guidance still applies before the edit moves into review.

DRAFTING & APPROVAL

Who can send a draft back? Who can release it?

I kept the content workspace separate from client records. Claude agents work with approved material in SharePoint. The advisor brings subject expertise, and the compliance agent flags language that needs support or qualification.

CRM HANDOFF

Carry the prospect’s question into the meeting.

A consultation request follows a separate path. The form passes the referral source, when available, and the service option the visitor clicked into Slant CRM. The meeting brief uses those structured fields to give the advisor a starting question.

Example · From service click to meeting brief

A visitor requests a meeting from a service comparison. Slant records the selected option with the request. The brief suggests asking, “What caught your attention about that service?” The advisor can then learn what the person actually needs. The marketing agent works from aggregate web signals and has no need for that prospect record.
