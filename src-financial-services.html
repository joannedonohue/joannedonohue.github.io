SONORAN RIDGE CAPITAL / FINANCIAL SERVICES

From a tax question
to an advisor conversation.

I built an intent-based marketing tool for a financial services firm in our portfolio at Sonoran Ridge Capital. The firm serves high-net-worth business owners with specialized tax-planning needs. The tool helps us understand which questions bring owners to our content, point them toward relevant advisory services, and give the wealth advisor context when an owner asks to meet.

Intent-based marketing · Answer engine optimization · Advisor matching

AUDIENCE INTENT & CONTENT STRATEGY

What brings a business owner to an advisor?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) starts with a question a prospective client might ask. I designed the CMO agent to trace that question through the firm’s YouTube videos, blog posts, and other publications. It reads the video transcripts to find the advisor’s original wording, then compares it with the written coverage. When traffic points to growing interest in a topic, the agent flags a specific edit for the advisor to review.

Example · A video answers a question the blog leaves open

Suppose referrals rise for a post about tax planning before a business sale. Clarity shows readers repeatedly clicking a passage about when to involve an advisor. The CMO agent finds a YouTube transcript where an advisor addresses that question. It proposes adding the excerpt and a link to request a related consultation. The advisor decides whether that next step fits someone still exploring a sale.

DRAFTING & APPROVAL

Who can send a draft back? Who can release it?

I kept the content workspace separate from client records. Claude agents work with approved material in SharePoint. The advisor brings subject expertise, and the compliance agent flags language that needs support or qualification.

CRM HANDOFF

Connect the owner’s interest with an advisor.

A consultation request follows a separate path. The form passes the referral source, when available, and the service option the visitor clicked into Slant CRM. The meeting brief uses those structured fields to give the advisor a starting question.

Example · From service click to meeting brief

An owner clicks a tax-planning service and requests a meeting. Slant records the selected option with the request. The advisor’s brief suggests opening with, “What prompted you to look into tax planning for your business now?” The advisor can then learn what the person actually needs. The marketing agent works from aggregate web signals and has no need for that prospect record.
